Tips and Hints

Description

Plan moves around uncovering cards and maintaining flexibility in the tableau.

How to use

  1. Look for moves that reveal a face-down card.
  2. Compare the effect of moving a card to a foundation with keeping it available in the tableau.
  3. Save a checkpoint before experimenting.

Note: You can recycle the waste as often as needed.

Tip: Prioritize emptying a column when you have a useful king to place there.

Warning: A legal move is not necessarily the best move; some deals cannot be won.

Limitations

These are general strategies, not an automated hint system or a guarantee of victory.

Example

Moving a sequence from a column with a hidden card may uncover more options than moving a single already exposed card.
