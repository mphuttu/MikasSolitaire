Rules and Moves

Description

Foundations build upwards by suit. Tableau columns build downwards in alternating red and black suits.

How to use

  1. Put an ace on an empty foundation, then add cards of the same suit in ascending order.
  2. Place a face-up card or a descending alternating-colour sequence on a card one rank higher and the opposite colour.
  3. Place only a king or a sequence beginning with a king on an empty column.
  4. Click the empty stock to recycle the waste.

Note: The last face-down card in a column turns face up automatically when uncovered. A foundation's top card can be moved back to the tableau.

Tip: Double-click a playable waste card or column top to find a legal foundation automatically.

Warning: Moving a card to a foundation too early can restrict tableau moves. You may move its top card back if legal.

Limitations

Only individual top cards go to foundations. Double-clicking an ineligible card does not move it; there is no automatic completion.

When no forward move remains, including after cycling the stock, the game offers to restart the deal or save it. The check excludes moves back from foundations and moving an entire king column to an empty column. You can dismiss the warning and still make those moves or undo.

Example

An exposed heart two can be added to a heart ace; it cannot be added to a diamond ace.
