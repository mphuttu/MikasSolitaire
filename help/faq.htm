Frequently Asked Questions

Description

Answers to common questions about playing and saving.

How to use

Choose the question that describes your situation.

Why does double-click do nothing?

Only a face-up top card in a tableau column or the waste can move to a foundation, and a legal destination must exist.

Can I replay the same deal?

Yes. Choose Game > Restart deal, including after opening a saved game.

Can I draw three cards or limit recycling?

No. This version draws one card and allows unlimited recycling.

Can I undo a move?

Yes. Choose Game > Undo or press Ctrl+Z. Draws and recycling can also be undone. Undo history is not saved.

Note: The six back designs are a per-user setting.

Tip: Use the help Contents or Index to locate related subjects.

Warning: Reopening a checkpoint replaces later unsaved moves after confirmation.

Limitations

No hint solver, game statistics or scoring is provided.

Example

Use Save as to preserve a useful starting position before trying another strategy.
