Getting Started

Description

Each deal starts with 28 cards in seven columns and 24 cards in the stock. The uppermost card of each column is face up.

How to use

  1. Choose Game > New game or press Ctrl+N.
  2. Click a face-up card or valid sequence, then click a destination column. Right-click to cancel the selection.
  3. Double-click the top card of a column or the waste to move it to a foundation when legal.
  4. Save with Ctrl+S and reopen with Ctrl+O.

Note: The waste is the pile immediately to the right of the stock. Its top card is playable.

Tip: Expose face-down cards by moving the cards above them.

Warning: Restarting a deal discards its current progress unless you saved it separately.

Limitations

Card movement uses clicks rather than drag-and-drop. Only the top card of the waste or a column can be sent to a foundation.

Example

Click a black seven, then a red eight in another column to move the seven and any valid sequence below it.
