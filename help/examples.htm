Examples

Description

These examples illustrate common legal moves and the foundation shortcut.

How to use

  1. Double-click an exposed club ace: it moves to an empty foundation.
  2. When the club two is the top card of the waste, double-click it to place it on that ace.
  3. Click a red queen followed by a black king to move the queen and any valid sequence below it.
  4. Click the stock; click it again when empty to recycle the waste.

Note: Suit and rank must match the next foundation card; red and black must alternate in the tableau.

Tip: If double-click does not move the card, check whether its foundation requires an earlier rank.

Warning: Double-click only moves an exposed top card, not an entire sequence to a foundation.

Limitations

These moves depend on the actual cards in the shuffled deal; the application does not generate a guaranteed move.

Another example

When a column is empty, move a king there to make room to uncover another card.
